After taking a booking product from idea to 2,000 active users, three lessons stand out.

## 1. Talk to users before choosing the stack

The first version solved a problem nobody had. A week of calls with real teams changed the whole roadmap.

## 2. Make the boring parts reliable

Reminders, time zones and double-booking checks matter more than any new feature.

## 3. Measure what users feel

Cutting load times in half did more for retention than any redesign.

- Track performance from day one.
- Ship small, ship often.
- Write down every decision.
